# Y Combinator Post-Money Safe (Valuation Cap, no Discount)

**This Safe is provided “as is.” The official form lives at ycombinator.com.**

Company: Halden Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”)

Investor: Meridian Ventures I, L.P. (the “Investor”)

Purchase Amount: $400,000 (the “Purchase Amount”)

Post-Money Valuation Cap: $8,000,000 (the “Post-Money Valuation Cap”)

Discount Rate: N/A (this version has no discount)

Purchase Date: November 10, 2026

## THE SECURITIES OFFERED HEREBY HAVE NOT BEEN REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933 OR ANY STATE SECURITIES LAWS AND ARE OFFERED AND SOLD IN RELIANCE ON EXEMPTIONS FROM THE REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS OF SAID ACT AND SUCH LAWS.

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 “Capital Stock”** means the Company’s common stock and preferred stock.

**1.2 “Change of Control”** means (i) a transaction or series of related transactions in which any person or group acquires beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting power of the Company’s Capital Stock, or (ii) a sale, lease, or other disposition of all or substantially all of the Company’s assets.

**1.3 “Company Capitalization”** means the sum of, as of immediately prior to the Equity Financing: (i) all shares of Capital Stock issued and outstanding; (ii) all outstanding Converting Securities; (iii) all (a) issued and outstanding options, and (b) issued and outstanding warrants, to acquire Capital Stock or Converting Securities; and (iv) the Unissued Option Pool; but excluding (A) this Safe, (B) all other Safes, and (C) convertible promissory notes.

**1.4 “Dissolution Event”** means a liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company.

**1.5 “Equity Financing”** means the first sale of Capital Stock by the Company to one or more investors in a transaction or series of related transactions, after the Purchase Date, with the primary purpose of raising capital, in which the Company is not a party to a merger or consolidation.

**1.6 “Liquidity Event”** means a Change of Control or a direct listing of the Company’s Capital Stock.

**1.7 “Safe Price”** means the Post-Money Valuation Cap divided by the Company Capitalization.

**1.8 “Unissued Option Pool”** means the difference between (i) the number of shares of Capital Stock reserved for issuance under the Company’s equity incentive plans immediately prior to the Equity Financing, and (ii) the number of shares of Capital Stock actually subject to outstanding options and warrants.

## 2. Purchase; Conversion

**2.1 Purchase.** On the Purchase Date the Investor agrees to purchase, and the Company agrees to sell, the Purchase Amount of the Safe.

**2.2 Conversion at Equity Financing.** In connection with an Equity Financing, the Company will automatically convert this Safe into the type of Capital Stock issued in the Equity Financing, at a conversion price equal to the lower of (i) the price per share of the Capital Stock sold in the Equity Financing, and (ii) the Safe Price.

**2.3 Conversion at Liquidity Event.** If there is a Liquidity Event before the Safe is converted, the Investor will, at its option, either (i) receive a cash payment equal to the Purchase Amount, or (ii) automatically convert this Safe into the type of Capital Stock that would be received in the Liquidity Event, at the Safe Price.

**2.4 Dissolution.** If there is a Dissolution Event before the Safe is converted, the Investor will receive a cash payment equal to the Purchase Amount, due and payable immediately prior to, or concurrent with, the payment of any proceeds to holders of Capital Stock, and junior to payment of the Company’s liabilities and any indebtedness that is senior.

## 3. Subsequent Issuances (this instrument is silent)

This Safe does not, by its terms, grant the Investor pro rata rights, preemptive rights, information rights, or a most-favored-nation right in respect of subsequent issuances. Any such rights, if any, exist only in a separate writing that is validly executed by the Company.

## 4. No Stockholder Rights

Until this Safe is converted, the Investor has no rights as a stockholder, including no voting rights, no information rights, and no inspection rights, except as required by law.

## 5. Transfer

The Investor may transfer this Safe only in compliance with applicable securities laws and the Company’s reasonable restrictions on assignment. The Company may not assign this Safe without the Investor’s prior written consent.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Governing Law.** This Safe is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**6.2 Entire Agreement.** This Safe, including the other documents referred to in this Safe, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, term sheets, and negotiations, whether written or oral, with respect to the subject matter hereof.

**6.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Safe may be amended only by a writing signed by the Company and the Investor. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

**6.4 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered to the addresses on the signature page (or a successor address given in writing).

**6.5 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder continues in effect.

**6.6 Counterparts.** This Safe may be executed in counterparts, including electronic.
