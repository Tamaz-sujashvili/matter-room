# Side Letter — Halden Analytics, Inc. / Meridian Ventures I, L.P.

**Draft — November 16, 2026 — not executed**

This Side Letter (this “Side Letter”) is entered into in connection with the Safe dated November 10, 2026 (the “Safe”) between Halden Analytics, Inc. (the “Company”) and Meridian Ventures I, L.P. (the “Investor”).

## 1. Recitals

**1.1** The parties entered into the Safe on the Purchase Date.

**1.2** The parties intend this Side Letter to **amend and complement** the Safe and to set out certain additional rights of the Investor.

**1.3** Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Safe.

## 2. Pro Rata Rights

**2.1** The Investor shall have the right to purchase its **Pro Rata Share** of any subsequent issuance of Capital Stock by the Company, until the closing of an Equity Financing.

**2.2** “Pro Rata Share” means the fraction the Investor would need to maintain its percentage ownership of the Company **as of the date of this Side Letter**.

**2.3** The Company shall give the Investor **written notice** of any such issuance. The Investor shall have a period of time to exercise, as reasonably agreed.

## 3. Information Rights

**3.1** Beginning on the date of this Side Letter, the Company shall deliver to the Investor:

- (a) **monthly** unaudited financial statements within fifteen (15) days after month-end;
- (b) an annual budget, approved by the board, before each fiscal year; and
- (c) such other information as the Investor may reasonably request.

**3.2** The Investor shall have inspection rights during normal business hours upon reasonable notice.

## 4. Most Favored Nation

**4.1** If, before an Equity Financing, the Company issues any subsequent Safe **or convertible promissory note** with terms more favorable to the holder than the Safe, the Investor shall automatically receive those more favorable terms.

**4.2** “More favorable” includes, without limitation, a lower valuation cap, a discount, a higher interest rate, more senior repayment, and additional rights.

## 5. Conflict; Entire Agreement

**5.1** This Side Letter and the Safe together constitute the entire agreement of the parties with respect to the subject matter of the investment.

**5.2** In the event of any conflict between this Side Letter and the Safe, **this Side Letter shall control**.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1** This Side Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

**6.2** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including electronic.

**6.3** This Side Letter binds successors and permitted assigns of the Safe.

**6.4** This Side Letter terminates on the earlier of an Equity Financing or a Liquidity Event, except that then-surviving information-rights periods, if any, continue as written.
