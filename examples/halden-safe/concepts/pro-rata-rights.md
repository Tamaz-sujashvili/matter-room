# Pro rata rights

The right to buy a slice of later issuances to hold a percentage. Four documents
say four different things about it, and the draft side letter says the widest.

## What each document says

| Document | Position | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Safe | Grants none; rights exist only in a separate signed writing | [[raw/safe.md#3-subsequent-issuances-this-instrument-is-silent]] |
| Term sheet (non-binding) | Pro rata "in subsequent Safes until Series Seed" | [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]] |
| Email 02 | "of course Meridian keeps pro rata going forward" and will be "in the side letter" | [[raw/email-02.md#email-02-13-nov-2026]] |
| Side letter §2.1 | Any subsequent **issuance of Capital Stock**, until the closing of an **Equity Financing** | [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]] |

## Four defects in the draft

1. **Scope.** §2.1 covers "**any subsequent issuance of Capital Stock**" —
   [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]]. The term sheet covered "subsequent
   Safes" — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]]. As drafted the right reaches the next
   priced round, option grants, and shares issued on conversion, none of which the
   term sheet put on the table. Read with no carve-out, every hiring grant becomes a
   notice event.
2. **Circular denominator.** §2.2 defines Pro Rata Share as the fraction needed to
   maintain "its percentage ownership... as of the date of this Side Letter" —
   [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]]. On that date the Investor owns nothing:
   the Safe has not converted and the Safe itself says it has no stockholder rights
   — [[raw/safe.md#4-no-stockholder-rights]]. The definition computes to zero.
3. **No price.** "The right to purchase" its share, with no statement of the price
   or terms of purchase — [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]]. Normal drafting
   says "at the price paid by the new investors".
4. **No clock.** The exercise period is "as reasonably agreed" —
   [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]]. A right with no fixed notice period and
   no election window cannot be exercised against a closing calendar.

## The duration point, honestly

The letter runs "until the closing of an Equity Financing"; the term sheet said
"until Series Seed" — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]]. These are closer than they
look. "Equity Financing" is defined as the first sale of Capital Stock to investors
with the primary purpose of raising capital — [[raw/safe.md#1-definitions]] — and a
Series Seed is such a sale. The gap only opens if the Company raises a priced round
that is not called a Series Seed: the letter's right would end there, the term
sheet's would not.

That cuts in the founder's favour, which is why it is a one-sentence clarification,
not a fight. Counsel's 16 November gloss — "we treat Series Seed as the Equity
Financing" — is not the definition and does not bind —
[[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]].

## Assessment

The right itself is normal for a $400k lead, and the founder conceded the principle
in substance. The draft's problem is that it grants a right whose size, price and
timing nobody can calculate, over a class of issuances nobody agreed to. Fix the
mechanics and the scope, keep the right.
