# Entire agreement and amendment

Two integration clauses point at each other. The Safe says it is the whole deal.
The side letter says it and the Safe together are the whole deal, and that the
side letter wins any conflict.

## The texts

- Safe §6.2: the Safe "constitutes the entire agreement... and supersedes all
  prior agreements, understandings, term sheets, and negotiations, whether written
  or oral" — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].
- Safe §6.3: amendable "only by a writing signed by the Company and the Investor"
  — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].
- Side letter §1.2: intended to "**amend and complement** the Safe" —
  [[raw/side-letter.md#1-recitals]].
- Side letter §5.1: the two documents together are the entire agreement —
  [[raw/side-letter.md#5-conflict-entire-agreement]].
- Side letter §5.2: "In the event of any conflict... **this Side Letter shall
  control**" — [[raw/side-letter.md#5-conflict-entire-agreement]].

## The reading that matters

§6.2 is a **temporal** clause. It supersedes what came *before* it: the 28 October
term sheet and the 6 November call — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]]. A side
letter signed on 20 November is not prior, so §6.2 does not void it.

A signed side letter is therefore effective, and it is effective by the route
§6.3 itself provides: a writing signed by both parties —
[[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].

That reading does **not** rescue §5.2. A letter dated ten days after the Safe
cannot be read to override a clause that was not in contemplation when the Safe
was signed, and §5.2's "shall control" is not limited to the terms the letter
actually addresses. It is the one clause in the bundle with no commercial purpose
beyond winning a later argument.

## What follows for the founder

1. Nothing in the term sheet or the emails binds her — all superseded, all
   non-binding or pre-contractual — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].
2. Everything she does sign on 20 November **does** bind her, and binds her over
   the Safe where the letter says so — [[raw/side-letter.md#5-conflict-entire-agreement]].
3. The scope of what she signs is therefore the entire negotiation. See
   [[what-does-the-founder-owe]].
