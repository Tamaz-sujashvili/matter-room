# Most favored nation (MFN)

If the Company later issues something better, the Investor's terms automatically
improve. The term sheet scoped it to Safes. The draft scopes it to Safes **or
convertible notes**. That difference is the single largest economic exposure in
the bundle.

## What each document says

- Term sheet: "**MFN on subsequent Safes** (notes not mentioned)" —
  [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]].
- Email 03: founder asks directly whether the side letter picks up notes, because
  a bridge note is planned — [[raw/email-03.md#email-03-14-nov-2026]].
- Email 04: "The letter picks up any subsequent Safe **or convertible promissory
  note**. We discussed this on the 6 November call." — [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]].
- Side letter §4.1: applies to any subsequent Safe **or convertible promissory
  note** issued before an Equity Financing —
  [[raw/side-letter.md#4-most-favored-nation]].
- Side letter §4.2: "more favorable" includes a lower cap, a discount, a higher
  interest rate, more senior repayment, and **additional rights** —
  [[raw/side-letter.md#4-most-favored-nation]].

## Why it is the worst clause in the bundle

1. **It reaches the bridge.** A note is the instrument a pre-seed company uses to
   buy six months. With §4.1 as drafted, any note with a lower cap or a discount
   resets Meridian's terms automatically, with no notice step and no election —
   [[raw/side-letter.md#4-most-favored-nation]].
2. **"Additional rights" is unbounded.** It imports any side letter term given to
   any later investor, including information and pro rata terms the Company
   negotiated separately — [[raw/side-letter.md#4-most-favored-nation]].
3. **The 6 November call does not carry it.** The Safe supersedes "all prior
   agreements, understandings, term sheets, and negotiations" —
   [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]]. A call on 6 November is a prior negotiation.
   The only thing that can grant an MFN over notes is the signed side letter.
4. **No cap, no floor, no notice.** Nothing in §4 requires the Company to tell the
   Investor it has triggered, or to paper the improvement.

## The trap in the concession

Counsel offers: limit the MFN to Safes and "we'll consider it — I'm not promising
we'll agree" — [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]]. That is an invitation to
negotiate, not an agreement. Do not record it as a win.

## Assessment

Scope, not existence, is the fight. An MFN over Safes is market for a lead at this
size. An MFN over notes plus "additional rights" is a permanent most-favoured
option on every future financing decision. See the redline for the exact cut:
[[safe-vs-side-letter]].
