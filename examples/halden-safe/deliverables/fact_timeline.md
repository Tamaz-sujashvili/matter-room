## What the dates actually do

Three dates are in play — 20, 23 and 30 November — and **none of them is a deadline
the Company has agreed to**. The only signed instrument is the Safe, and the Safe
names one date: the Purchase Date, 10 November —
[[raw/safe.md#1-definitions]]. The 30 November outside date sits in a term sheet
that says it is non-binding — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]].

Counsel's 20 November ask is a scheduling preference with a social cost attached
("I need a reason") — [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]]. It is not a default,
not a termination right, and not a condition to funding. See
[[deliverables/research_memo]] §5.

- **6 November call.** Counsel says the MFN over notes was discussed then —

  bundle records it. The founder's 14 November email treats notes as **not**
  agreed — [[raw/email-03.md#email-03-14-nov-2026]]. The Safe supersedes prior oral
  negotiations in terms — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].
- **Information-rights timing.** Term sheet says at Series Seed, "not before" —
  [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]]. Counsel reads the same words as a floor —
  [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]]. Both readings are of the same clause;
  the clause does not resolve them, and it is not binding either way.
