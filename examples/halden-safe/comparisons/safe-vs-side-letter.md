# Safe vs side letter — clause by clause

The Safe is executed and silent on investor rights. The side letter is a draft and
loud about them. Where they differ:

| Topic | Safe (executed 10 Nov) | Side letter (draft 16 Nov) | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro rata | None; separate writing required — [[raw/safe.md#3-subsequent-issuances-this-instrument-is-silent]] | Any issuance of Capital Stock until Equity Financing; circular share; no price; no clock — [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]] | New right, wider scope, broken mechanics |
| Information | None before conversion — [[raw/safe.md#4-no-stockholder-rights]] | Monthly within 15 days, budget, on-request, inspection — [[raw/side-letter.md#3-information-rights]] | New covenant, no end date |
| MFN | None | Safes **and notes**, plus "additional rights" — [[raw/side-letter.md#4-most-favored-nation]] | New right, wider than term sheet |
| Stockholder rights | None until conversion — [[raw/safe.md#4-no-stockholder-rights]] | Inspection rights — [[raw/side-letter.md#3-information-rights]] | Partial reversal |
| Integration | Safe is the whole deal; prior terms superseded — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]] | Both documents are the whole deal; letter controls conflicts — [[raw/side-letter.md#5-conflict-entire-agreement]] | Competing clauses |
| Amendment | Signed writing of both parties — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]] | Silent on how the letter itself is amended | Gap |

## The conflict clause is the hinge

Every row above is a new right, and each one is effective only because the letter
would be signed after the Safe. §6.2 reaches backwards, so it kills the term sheet
and the 6 November call, not this letter — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].

What §5.2 adds is precedence: on any conflict the letter controls —
[[raw/side-letter.md#5-conflict-entire-agreement]]. That is what lets §3.1 override
"no information rights" in §4 of the Safe. Remove §5.2 and the same letter still
grants the same rights; it just stops claiming to win every unaddressed argument.
[[entire-agreement]] has the full reading.

## What the Investor would lose if §5.2 went

Nothing it actually asked for. Section 2, 3 and 4 of the letter grant the rights
directly. §5.2 is belt and braces for a fight nobody has had yet — which is exactly
why it is cheap for the founder to remove and cheap for counsel to keep.
