# Term sheet vs side letter — where the deal moved

The term sheet is dated 28 October and is non-binding except for confidentiality
and expenses — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]]. The side letter is dated
16 November. Three of its four terms moved after the term sheet, and all three
moved toward the Investor.

| Term | Term sheet (28 Oct) | Emails (12–16 Nov) | Side letter (16 Nov) | Moved? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pro rata | Until Series Seed — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]] | "of course... going forward" — [[raw/email-02.md#email-02-13-nov-2026]] | Any issuance of Capital Stock, until **Equity Financing** — [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]] | Yes, wider in scope |
| Information rights | At Series Seed, not before — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]] | "floor, not the ceiling" — [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]] | Monthly from day one — [[raw/side-letter.md#3-information-rights]] | Yes, accelerated |
| MFN scope | Subsequent Safes — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]] | Founder flags notes — [[raw/email-03.md#email-03-14-nov-2026]] | Safes **or notes** — [[raw/side-letter.md#4-most-favored-nation]] | Yes, wider |
| Cap | $8M post-money — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]] | "not trying to renegotiate the cap" — [[raw/email-03.md#email-03-14-nov-2026]] | Silent | No |
| Closing | On or before 30 Nov — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]] | Funding 23 Nov; letter by 20 Nov — [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]] | Silent | Compressed by demand, not by contract |

## The pattern

Every movement is in the same direction and none is acknowledged as a movement.
Counsel's framing is that the letter merely restates what was agreed
("as discussed", "we discussed this on the 6 November call") —
[[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]]. The documents say otherwise: on all three
points the letter grants more than the term sheet recorded.

## Why the term sheet still matters

Not as an obligation — it is non-binding and it was superseded by the Safe's
integration clause — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]]. It matters as **evidence of
the parties' shared understanding on 28 October**, which is the natural benchmark
for a court or an arbitrator asked what "additional rights" or "Equity Financing"
was meant to mean.

That is the founder's real argument: not "you promised me X", but "the deal we
priced included Y, and the letter now says Y plus three".

## The one movement in her favour

Email 04 concedes the point that the exercise mechanics can be left loose:
"Notice and exercise period are 'as reasonably agreed' for now" —
[[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]]. It reads as a concession and it is the
opposite: it puts an indefinite option into the letter. See [[pro-rata-rights]].
