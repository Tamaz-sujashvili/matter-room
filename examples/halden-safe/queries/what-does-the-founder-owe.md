# What does the founder actually owe?

Filed 18 November 2026, before close. The question the memo answers.

> The emails promise pro rata and information rights "of course". The Safe is
> silent and has an entire-agreement clause. The side letter grants both but
> conflicts with the Safe on MFN timing and scope. What does the founder owe on
> Monday, what gets redlined before Friday, and what stays off the Safe?

## Answer, short form

1. **Today she owes what the Safe says and nothing else.** $400,000 converting at
   the lower of the financing price or an $8M cap; no pro rata, no information
   rights, no MFN — [[raw/safe.md#3-subsequent-issuances-this-instrument-is-silent]].
2. **The term sheet binds her to nothing** except confidentiality and expenses,
   and the Safe's integration clause supersedes it outright —
   [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]], [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]].
3. **The emails bind her to nothing either**, but they are admissions she will
   have to live with in the negotiation — [[raw/email-02.md#email-02-13-nov-2026]].
4. **What she signs on Friday binds her**, over the Safe, wherever the letter says
   it controls — [[raw/side-letter.md#5-conflict-entire-agreement]].
5. **So the answer is not in the documents. It is in the redline.**

## The three things that must change before signature

| # | Change | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delete §5.2 (letter controls conflicts) | Otherwise every unaddressed term in the Safe loses silently — [[entire-agreement]] |
| 2 | Narrow MFN §4.1 to Safes; delete "additional rights" from §4.2 | It otherwise captures a bridge note and every future side letter — [[mfn]] |
| 3 | Fix pro rata §2.2 and §2.3; set the duration | A circular share and no clock is a right nobody can exercise — [[pro-rata-rights]] |

Information rights (§3) is a commercial trade, not a drafting error: take a
confidentiality clause and a sunset, or make the update non-contractual —
[[information-rights]].

## What stays off the Safe

Nothing is added to the Safe. It is the market-standard YC form, it is already
executed, and every investor-specific right belongs in the side letter. Amending
the Safe would need both signatures under §6.3 and would buy nothing the letter
does not already do — [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].
