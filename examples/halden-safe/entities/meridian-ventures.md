# Meridian Ventures I, L.P.

Lead investor. $400,000 on a YC Post-Money Safe at an $8,000,000 post-money
valuation cap, no discount — [[raw/safe.md#1-definitions]], [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]].

## What it holds now

- An executed Safe dated 10 November 2026. Before conversion it has no stockholder
  rights, no information rights, no inspection rights — [[raw/safe.md#4-no-stockholder-rights]].
- No pro rata right, no MFN, no information right under the Safe itself. The
  instrument says so in terms — [[raw/safe.md#3-subsequent-issuances-this-instrument-is-silent]].

## What it is asking for

Pro rata, monthly reporting, inspection rights, and an MFN that reaches
convertible notes. All of it sits in a draft side letter dated 16 November 2026,
unexecuted — [[raw/side-letter.md#1-recitals]].

The ask is not frivolous: counsel stated on 13 November that the side letter
*is* the deal — [[raw/email-02.md#email-02-13-nov-2026]]. The question for the
founder is what that concession costs, not whether it was promised:
[[pro-rata-rights]].
