# Elena Marsh

CEO and founder of [[halden-analytics]]. Holds 8,000,000 of 10,000,000 shares
before conversion — [[raw/cap-table.md#outstanding]]. Signs for the Company on
both the Safe and the side letter.

## Her stated position

Written to opposing counsel on 14 November, before the draft arrived — [[raw/email-03.md#email-03-14-nov-2026]]:

1. Information rights belong at Series Seed, per the term sheet. She will send a
   voluntary monthly update but will not accept a reporting covenant at pre-seed.
2. MFN was limited to subsequent Safes in the term sheet. She wants to know now
   if the side letter picks up convertible notes, because a bridge note is
   planned.
3. She is not reopening the cap. Her line is that the side letter must not become
   a second term sheet.

## Why her position is weaker than it looks

Both asks were made against a **non-binding** term sheet — [[raw/term-sheet.md#investment]].
Counsel answered the second one by widening the MFN and answering the first by
reading "at Series Seed" as a floor — [[raw/email-04.md#email-04-16-nov-2026]].
Her 14 November email answers neither point with a redline; it states a preference
and offers a voluntary update instead — [[raw/email-03.md#email-03-14-nov-2026]].
Against a signed letter, a preference is not a position: [[information-rights]], [[mfn]].

She did not dispute pro rata at all. Her 14 November email challenges only
information rights and MFN scope, so on pro rata she has already conceded the
shape of the right and only the mechanics remain: [[pro-rata-rights]].
