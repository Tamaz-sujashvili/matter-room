## Disagreements carried into the memo

### D1 — Does the Safe's integration clause kill the side letter?

- **Consistency seat:** yes. The letter papers a 6 November understanding, therefore
  a prior understanding, therefore superseded by §6.2 —
  [[raw/safe.md#6-miscellaneous]].
- **Chairman:** no. §6.2 governs what came before it; a letter signed on 20 November
  is after, and §6.3 supplies the amendment mechanism. The consistency seat's
  reading would also make its own §5.2 analysis pointless — if §6.2 voided the
  letter, §5.2 would never be reached.
- **Memo treatment:** recorded as a live risk, resolved against the consistency
  seat, with the reason stated.

### D2 — Is §2.2 void or merely unworkable?

- **Ambiguity seat:** void for uncertainty; the right fails.
- **Chairman:** unworkable and construed against the drafter. A court asked to
  enforce a financing document will read a circular definition into a workable one
  before it voids a clause the Investor's own counsel drafted —
  [[raw/side-letter.md#2-pro-rata-rights]].
- **Memo treatment:** disagreement kept. The actionable point is the same either
  way: fix the definition, do not litigate it.

### D3 — Which clause ranks first?

- **Risk seat:** MFN first (money), information second.
- **Missing seat:** pro rata first (administrative permanence).
- **Chairman:** both. MFN ranks first on exposure and pro rata ranks first on repair
  priority, because its defects are cured by four sentences and the MFN's are cured
  by a negotiation. The memo states the exposure order and says so.

### D4 — When to concede monthly reporting

- **Risk seat:** trade it now for MFN narrowing.
- **Consistency seat:** concede nothing until §5.2 is out, because §5.2 makes every
  concession permanent.
- **Chairman:** the consistency seat is right on sequence. Delete §5.2 first, then
  trade reporting for MFN scope. Same trade, different order, materially different
  outcome.
